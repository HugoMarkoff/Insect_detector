About

A wildlife trap board repurposed as an insect timelapse camera. An ATmega328P wakes a Raspberry Pi on a fixed interval, fires an infrared illuminator, captures a frame with a NoIR camera, and pushes it here — no motion trigger, just steady frames.

Build

Really a Robot Trap PCB V3.3 · ATmega328P · Raspberry Pi · IMX708 NoIR.
Source & flashing docs: github.com/HugoMarkoff/Insect_detector
