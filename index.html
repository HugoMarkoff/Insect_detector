A wildlife-trap board (ATmega328P) wakes a Raspberry Pi on a fixed interval, fires an infrared illuminator, and captures a frame — no motion trigger, just steady frames. "Insect" frames are flagged by frame-to-frame change detection on the Pi (a motion proxy on the static scene).
